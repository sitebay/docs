# Visemes Explained

A viseme is a mouth shape used for speech animation. When an avatar has the right visemes, SiteBay can match the face to the spoken audio instead of only opening and closing the mouth.

## Why They Matter

Speech contains sounds such as "f", "o", and "m" that need different mouth shapes. A model with proper visemes can form those shapes. A model without them can still move, but the result looks less natural.

## Badge Meaning

- **Excellent:** standard speech visemes are present.
- **Good:** compatible facial blend shapes are present.
- **Acceptable:** basic jaw movement is available.
- **None:** no useful mouth controls were found.

## What To Look For

When choosing a custom avatar, look for model descriptions that mention lip sync, visemes, ARKit, facial blend shapes, mouth shapes, or speech animation.

If your avatar imports but mouth movement looks weak, choose a model with a stronger badge or re-export it from a character tool with facial blend shapes enabled.
