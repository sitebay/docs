# Notebooks

Stop pasting screenshots of charts into Slack. PostHog Notebooks on SiteBay let your team collaborate directly on your analytics data.

Think of it like Google Docs, but wired directly into your site's live data feed.

## Getting Started

1. Head to **Analytics > Notebooks > New** in your SiteBay dashboard.
2. Give it a name (like "Why did sales tank last Tuesday?").
3. Drop in live charts, session replays, and text blocks to build your case.
4. Save it and share the link with your team.

## Why They're Awesome

- **Live Data**: Your charts update automatically. No more stale data.
- **Team Comments**: Tag your developers or marketers right on a specific data point.
- **AI Integration**: Ask the **SiteBay MCP Server** to summarize a notebook, or use the **SiteClaw** app to read a generated report of the notebook's findings while you're on the train.

## Typical Use Cases

| Use Case | Example |
|-----|---------|
| **Deep Dives** | "Q4 Black Friday Conversion Analysis" |
| **Weekly Syncs** | Building a standard weekly metrics review doc. |
| **Bug Hunts** | Tying a bunch of error logs and session replays together for your devs. |
| **Client Handoffs** | Giving stakeholders a clean, easy-to-read summary of their site's performance. |
