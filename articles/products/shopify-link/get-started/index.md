Connect the intended Shopify store through the authorized SiteBay linking flow, then verify its relationship with the selected site.

## Authorize the store

Use a Shopify account permitted to install or authorize the integration. Review the requested scopes and return to SiteBay after authorization. Confirm the linked store identity; an open Shopify tab does not establish a completed connection.

## Configure the site link

Select the linked store and review the site's blog or theme-link settings. A saved connection and a successfully applied theme change are different outcomes.

## Test the result

Open the relevant storefront and content links. Verify navigation and preview behavior without placing a real order. Shopify commerce data and SiteBay WordPress content remain owned by their respective systems.

For direct theme work, read [Shopify themes]({{< relref "themes/shopify/index.md" >}}). Check store permissions when an operation is refused rather than switching to a more privileged credential without review.
