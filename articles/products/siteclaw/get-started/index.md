# SiteClaw: Mobile App & ChatGPT Assistant

**SiteClaw** is your portable, AI-powered control center for the SiteBay platform. It is available both as a dedicated mobile application for iOS and Android, and as an official custom GPT in the ChatGPT store. SiteClaw lets you manage your WordPress infrastructure from anywhere using natural voice and chat interactions.

Whether you are using the mobile app's 3D avatar or the ChatGPT interface, SiteClaw bridges the gap between natural language intent and complex platform operations, allowing you to manage your sites without ever opening a traditional dashboard.

## SiteClaw Mobile App

With a fully integrated 3D avatar that visualizes the assistant's personality and responses, the SiteClaw mobile app makes managing complex cloud infrastructure as easy as having a conversation.

### Key Mobile Features

- **Voice & Chat Assistance:** Talk to your infrastructure. Ask questions about site performance, create new staging environments, or trigger deployments using your voice.
- **AI Agent Visualization:** Interact with your personal 3D assistant avatar. You can customize its appearance and personality in the built-in **Studio**.
- **Real-Time Dashboards:** View site status, performance metrics, and PostHog analytics directly within the app.
- **Embedded Web Views:** Access **Code Server** (IDE) and your **Staging** environments without leaving the app.
- **Checkpoint Timeline:** Review a history of all actions taken on your sites, providing a clear audit trail of changes.
- **PostHog Integration:** Native analytics and event tracking to monitor your app's performance and user engagement.

### Getting Started on Mobile

1.  **Download the App:** Install SiteClaw from the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/) or [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/).
2.  **Sign In:** Use your SiteBay credentials to authenticate.
3.  **Link Your Assistant:** Once signed in, you can start interacting with your default assistant or create a custom one in the Studio.

## SiteClaw ChatGPT Assistant

While the **SiteBay MCP Server** empowers users of Anthropic's Claude, SiteBay also provides native integrations for the OpenAI ecosystem. **SiteClaw** is an official custom GPT available in the ChatGPT store, designed to be your conversational WordPress infrastructure manager.

### Accessing the ChatGPT Assistant

The SiteClaw ChatGPT Assistant is available to all ChatGPT Plus, Team, and Enterprise users.
*   **Link:** [SiteClaw by SiteBay](https://chat.openai.com/g/g-OUIbMa4mL-siteclaw-by-site-bay)
*   **Authentication:** The first time you interact with SiteClaw on ChatGPT, it will prompt you to securely link your SiteBay account via OAuth.

### Capabilities

SiteClaw leverages SiteBay's comprehensive API to perform a wide array of tasks from ChatGPT:

#### 1. Site Creation and Cloning
You can spin up new infrastructure instantly:
> *"Create a new staging site for mydomain.com and clone the production database over to it."*
> *"Spin up a new WordPress instance using the 'E-commerce Starter' template."*

#### 2. Design and Theming (Vision AI)
By combining ChatGPT's vision capabilities with the **SiteBay AI-Friendly Theme**, SiteClaw can perform "Image-to-Theme" translations:
> *(Upload a screenshot of a modern landing page)*
> *"Update my site's CSS tokens to match the color palette and typography of this screenshot."*
SiteClaw will analyze the image, extract the hex codes and design system, and apply them via the theme's utility classes.

#### 3. Analytics and Data Interrogation
SiteClaw is deeply hooked into SiteBay's PostHog integration:
> *"What were my top performing pages yesterday?"*
> *"Show me a summary of JavaScript errors affecting the checkout flow, and provide a link to the latest Session Replay."*
> *"Create a feature flag named 'new-hero-banner' and enable it for 20% of traffic in Canada."*

#### 4. Routine Maintenance and Backups
> *"List all outdated plugins on mysite.org."*
> *"Restore mysite.org to the backup from March 12th at 8:00 AM."*
> *"Give me the WP admin login URL and securely generate a one-time password."*

## Why Use SiteClaw?

SiteClaw abstracts away the technical complexity of hosting. It is ideal for agency owners managing multiple clients, marketers who need quick analytics without navigating complex dashboards, and developers who prefer to execute infrastructure commands via rapid chat context rather than writing scripts. 

The Mobile App, the ChatGPT Assistant, and the **SiteBay MCP Server** work seamlessly together, providing a unified, AI-native management experience regardless of your preferred interface.
