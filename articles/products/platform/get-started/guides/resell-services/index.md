Separate client access, application operations, and billing responsibilities before managing a site for someone else.

## Agree on ownership

Record who owns the SiteBay team, domain, repository, content, payment method, and external integrations. Use individual memberships rather than a shared owner login.

## Define the service

State which updates, backups, incident response, and application work you will perform. Do not promise an uptime, support response time, or reseller discount that is not part of the applicable agreement.

## Operate with clear targets

Select the client's team and site for each action. Verify staging changes before publication and retain the operation result. Keep one client's credentials and data out of another client's workspace.

## Plan a handover

Document how the client can receive ownership, exports, and access when the engagement ends. Review the current [team and billing workflow]({{< relref "products/platform/teams-and-billing/index.md" >}}) rather than assuming deleting a site cancels every subscription.
