Read the invoice and provider payment status before attempting another payment.

## Procedure

Use the payment action actually offered by the provider. Check the currency, amount and invoice reference before authorizing it. Do not assume every account is charged within a fixed number of hours or that all invoices use the same collection method.

## Verify and retain evidence

Refresh the invoice after the provider returns. Distinguish paid, pending, failed and an authorization hold; retain the receipt. If the first attempt is unresolved, check its state before submitting another charge.
