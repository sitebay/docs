Account closure, subscription cancellation, site deletion, and team departure are different actions. Do not delete a WordPress site just to stop a subscription, or assume that leaving a team closes the owner's account.

## Preserve what you need

Identify the teams and sites affected. Export the content and database you are authorized to retain, verify the backups outside the account being closed, and record any domains and external provider relationships. Site deletion is destructive and can remove staging with the live site.

## Resolve billing separately

Review the current subscription and its effective cancellation date with the billing provider. Use [stop billing]({{< relref "products/platform/billing/guides/stop-billing/index.md" >}}) rather than relying on a missing active site as evidence of cancellation. Keep receipts and any pending-payment or cancellation confirmation.

## Request the right closure

Use the account closure flow offered by the current product, or [contact support]({{< relref "products/platform/get-started/guides/support/index.md" >}}) with a clearly scoped closure request when that flow is not available. Confirm what data, access, sites and provider relationships will be removed before authorizing it. Review the account’s refund terms, data-retention policy, deletion schedule, and final charges before closure.

After confirmation, retain the provider and support records needed to verify completion. Never send support a plaintext password or recovery secret.
