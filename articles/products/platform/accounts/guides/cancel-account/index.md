Ready to head out? Closing your account shuts down all your WordPress sites and Kubernetes resources immediately. Any final balance will be charged to your card right away.

{{< note >}}
**7-day guarantee**: If you cancel within 7 days of signing up, you get a full refund. Just email support@sitebay.org or drop a note in the cancellation form.
{{< /note >}}

## How to close it down

1. Head over to [my.sitebay.org](https://my.sitebay.org), click **Account**, then **Settings**.
2. Find the **Close Account** panel and hit the **Close Account** button.
3. Punch in your username. Tell us why you're leaving if you want.
4. Click **Close Account** one last time.

Boom, done. All your services are wiped immediately, and you won't be able to log back in. 

## Just want to pause?

If you just want to stop getting billed but want to keep your account open, you can just [Remove Services](/docs/products/platform/billing/guides/stop-billing/) instead.
