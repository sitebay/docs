## The Complete Model Breakdown

### **GPT-3.5 Turbo: The Budget Baseline**
**Context:** 16K tokens | **Cost:** $0.0005 → $0.0015 per 1K tokens

**The Reality:** It's cheap, but you get what you pay for. GPT-3.5 struggles with complex reasoning and tends to lose track of instructions in multi-step tasks. Fine for simple interactions, but frustrating for anything sophisticated.

**Best For:** High-volume, simple tasks where cost is the only concern.

### **GPT-4 (8K/32K): The Original Powerhouse**
**Context:** 8K-32K tokens | **Cost:** $0.03 → $0.12 per 1K tokens

**The Reality:** Excellent reasoning but expensive and limited context. The 32K version costs twice as much for 4x the memory. These models feel outdated now that better options exist.

**Best For:** Legacy applications where you need GPT-4 quality but can't access newer models.

### **GPT-4 Turbo: The Balanced Performer**
**Context:** 128K tokens | **Cost:** $0.01 → $0.03 per 1K tokens

**The Reality:** Strong reasoning with massive context at 3x lower cost than GPT-4. This was the gold standard until GPT-4o arrived. Still excellent for demanding applications.

**Best For:** High-complexity tasks where cost is secondary to performance.

### **GPT-4o: The Premium Choice**
**Context:** 128K tokens | **Cost:** $0.005 → $0.015 per 1K tokens

**The Reality:** Matches GPT-4 Turbo's intelligence while being faster and cheaper. Adds multimodal capabilities (images, audio). If you can get access, it's compelling for high-end applications.

**Best For:** Premium applications requiring multimodal input and maximum performance.

### **o4-mini: The Game Changer** ⭐
**Context:** 200K tokens | **Cost:** $0.0011 → $0.0044 per 1K tokens

**The Reality:** This is where everything changes. Better reasoning than GPT-3.5, massive 200K context window (larger than GPT-4 Turbo), multimodal capabilities, and delivers incredible value. It's not just incrementally better—it's transformational.

**Best For:** Almost everything. Seriously.

### **O3/O3-Mini: The Reasoning Specialists**
**Context:** 200K tokens | **Cost:** Premium (undisclosed)

**The Reality:** These are specialized tools for complex reasoning tasks. O3 can "think" through problems step-by-step and use tools autonomously. Impressive but expensive and slow.

**Best For:** Specialized applications requiring maximum reasoning capability.
