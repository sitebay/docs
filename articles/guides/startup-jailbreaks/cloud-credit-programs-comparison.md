Compare a cloud-credit program against your architecture, eligibility, and expected cost after credits expire.

| Program | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| [AWS Activate](https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/) | Review the self-funded and provider-backed routes; the latter requires an eligible provider relationship |
| [Google for Startups Cloud Program](https://cloud.google.com/startup) | Select the route for your funding stage and review any AI-specific conditions |
| [Microsoft for Startups](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups) | Review current entry requirements and benefits for your startup |

## Check the award, not the headline

Advertised maximums are not automatic grants. Record the awarded amount, eligible services, activation deadline, expiry, previous-credit restrictions, and billing account.

## Model the transition

Estimate the normal monthly bill before applying credits. Include storage, transfer, database, support, and third-party services. Check which costs are excluded from the award.

## Apply accurately

Use your real company and funding details. Do not submit through an accelerator, investor, or organization identifier you are not entitled to use.

## Set a review date

Review usage before the expiry date and confirm the continuing cost with the provider. Keep a migration or downsizing plan for services that would exceed your budget.
