Choose offers that support a project you intend to build. A credit balance, free subscription, and recurring free tier are different benefits.

## Start with eligibility

Use the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) to check your student verification and each partner's current terms. Partner offers can have their own account, age, location, activation, and renewal requirements.

## Compare the actual benefit

| Offer type | Check before activation |
| --- | --- |
| Cloud credits | Eligible services, expiry, overages, and billing account |
| Developer subscription | Included features, usage limits, and renewal conditions |
| Domain or certificate | Initial term, renewal cost, and transfer restrictions |
| Hosting plan | Site limits, storage, traffic, and the upgrade path |

An advertised value is not the amount you will save. Count only the services your project needs, and check whether spending continues after the benefit expires.

## Use a small test project

Create a budget or usage alert where the provider supports one. Confirm what the account will charge after the offer ends. An alert is not necessarily a spending cap.

For SiteBay, inspect the plan and limits shown for your team. Verify any student benefit and its limits before activation. Read [SiteBay plans]({{< relref "guides/quick-answers/sitebay-essentials/sitebay-plans/index.md" >}}).

## Keep the project portable

Keep source code, necessary data exports, and account ownership under your control. Before an offer expires, decide whether to continue paying, reduce usage, or migrate. Remove resources you no longer need rather than assuming an expired credit balance stops them.
