Connect your Shopify store to your WordPress blog. Get PostHog analytics (session recordings, heatmaps, event logging) across both.

## Why Link?

- Single domain for SEO (blog + store)
- Unified PostHog analytics
- Better than Shopify's "Buy Button"

## Setup

1. **Prepare Shopify store** - Create one at shopify.com if needed

2. **Install SiteBay app**
   - Log into Shopify admin
   - Go to **Sales Channels**
   - Install **SiteBay WordPress** app

3. **Link sites**
   - You'll be redirected to My SiteBay
   - Select your WordPress site
   - Confirm link

## Result

- Blog at `/a/blog`
- Session recordings
- Surveys
- Advanced analytics
