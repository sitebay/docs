# AI Dashboard

Sitebay's AI Dashboard analyzes your website data and gives you actionable recommendations.

## What It Does

- **Auto-detects problems**: Traffic drops, slow pages, broken funnels
- **Predicts issues**: Warns you before bandwidth limits, seasonal spikes
- **Recommends fixes**: Specific actions with expected impact

## Key Features

### Smart Alerts
- Traffic anomalies (±20% from baseline)
- Performance degradation
- Conversion rate changes
- Competitor activity

### Weekly Reports
Delivered every Monday:
- Top metrics summary
- Problems detected
- Prioritized action items with time estimates

### Predictive Analytics
- Seasonal traffic forecasts
- Customer behavior patterns
- Revenue projections

## Setup

1. Go to **Dashboard > AI**
2. Set your business goals (sales, leads, engagement)
3. Add competitor URLs (optional)
4. Wait 1 week for baseline data

## Example Insights

| Problem | AI Finding | Recommendation |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Low mobile conversions | Checkout button below fold | Move button up (+35% expected) |
| Cart abandonment | Shipping cost surprise | Show costs upfront |
| Traffic drop | Competitor launched sale | Consider matching offer |

## When to Trust AI vs Your Judgment

**Trust AI for**: Data patterns, technical issues, timing optimization

**Use judgment for**: Brand decisions, major pivots, creative choices
