Choose a template from the available catalog, create a site, and replace its sample content.

Review the selected provider and team before creation. After provisioning completes, open the site and test the pages and integrations you intend to use.

The [template setup guide]({{< relref "products/templates/get-started-with-templates/index.md" >}}) covers selection, creation, customization, and verification.
