# Subscribe to SiteBay Status Updates

We build SiteBay on a rock-solid Kubernetes foundation, but the internet is wild and things happen. When we perform maintenance or if something goes sideways, we want you to be the first to know.

That's what the SiteBay Status Page is for. Get real-time updates on system health, scheduled maintenance, and incident reports straight to your inbox, phone, or feed reader.

### How to Subscribe

1. Head over to the [SiteBay Status Page](https://status.sitebay.org).
2. Hit the **Subscribe to Updates** button.
3. Pick how you want to be notified: Email, SMS, or Atom/RSS feed.

### Why You Should Care

- **Zero Surprises**: Get instant alerts if there's a service hiccup.
- **Plan Your Ops**: We'll give you a heads-up before any major maintenance on our Kubernetes clusters, so you can plan your own updates or campaigns accordingly.
- **Stay in the Loop**: If our automated systems or the SiteBay MCP Server detect platform-wide anomalies, we post it there.

Keep an eye on the status page to ensure your AI-native WordPress experience is running at peak performance. Subscribe today and never be out of the loop.
