# Monitoring

Want to know exactly what's going on with your WordPress sites? Monitoring isn't just a nice-to-have; it's how you make sure your stuff actually works. At SiteBay, our AI-native Kubernetes platform gives you the observability you need to keep your sites running hot.

### Why You Need It

- **Performance**: Slow sites lose visitors. We hook into tools like PostHog so you can track real user metrics and see exactly where the bottlenecks are.
- **Security**: With SiteClaw backing you up, you get real-time vulnerability scanning and automated threat blocking.
- **Uptime**: If your site sneezes, you should know before your users do.

### How We Do It

Because SiteBay is built on Kubernetes, we have deep insight into the health of your containers, database, and network traffic. 

- **PostHog Integration**: Dive deep into product analytics and session replays. See what your users are actually doing and where they're getting stuck.
- **SiteClaw**: This is your automated security guard. It monitors for outdated plugins, suspicious traffic, and known vulnerabilities, neutralizing threats before they take your site offline.
- **The Dashboard**: Head over to your SiteBay dashboard to see the high-level metrics. It's fast, punchy, and gives you the exact data you need without the fluff.

Don't guess if your site is healthy. Turn on your integrations, check your dashboard, and let SiteBay handle the heavy lifting.
