## Email Addresses and Contact Info

Listen, the email and contact info you use when signing up? It actually matters. That's how we ping you about updates, billing, and system alerts from our Kubernetes clusters. Keep it real and up to date, so you don't miss out on important platform news or PostHog dashboard notifications.

## Passwords

Let's talk about keeping your stuff locked down.

- **Strong Passwords:** Make them tough. Mix letters, numbers, and symbols. Don't use "password". You know the drill.
- **Mix It Up:** Your SiteBay dashboard password needs to be different from your WordPress admin passwords. Don't use the same key for every lock.
- **We Don’t Hold the Keys:** We don't store your plain-text passwords. If you lose your WordPress admin password, we can't magically recover it—though you can totally ask your AI agent via the **SiteBay MCP Server** to run a quick `wp user update` command for you.
- **Suspect Something? Change It:** If you think someone's poking around your account, change your password immediately.

## Setting and Resetting Your Password

- **Setting Up:** When you spin up a new site, you'll set up a password.
- **Forgot Your Password?:** It happens. If it's your main SiteBay account, reset it right from the login page. For your WordPress site, use the "Lost your password?" link on your WP login screen, or just fire up the **SiteClaw** mobile app and have the ChatGPT assistant reset it for you on the fly.

## Security Tips

- **Keep It Private:** Don't share your password.
- **Update Regularly:** Change your passwords once in a while.
- **Use a Password Manager:** Seriously, just use one. It makes life way easier and keeps your digital identity secure.

Stay safe out there. If you run into issues, SiteBay support is ready to help you out.
