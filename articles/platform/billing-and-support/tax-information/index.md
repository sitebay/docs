Use the tax information on the actual checkout or invoice for the transaction. The old country/province table on this page was not a maintained tax determination and must not be used to calculate what a particular customer owes.

## Check the billing facts

Confirm the customer billing details, currency, invoice period, tax lines and final total. A suggested currency in the public pricing catalog is not a tax-residency assessment. A quoted plan amount and an invoice total are not interchangeable.

## Correct a discrepancy

Record the invoice identifier and the specific billing field or tax line that appears wrong. Update the contact information through the billing provider where supported, then ask about a correction for the affected invoice. Do not assume changing an address retroactively changes a finalized invoice or qualifies a purchase for an exemption.

## Keep an auditable record

Retain the finalized invoice and any corrected version. For legal tax treatment or eligibility questions, consult the relevant authority or a qualified adviser using the actual transaction details. For a SiteBay billing record, [contact support]({{< relref "products/platform/get-started/guides/support/index.md" >}}) without sending complete card details or credentials.
