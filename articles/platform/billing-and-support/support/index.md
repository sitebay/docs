We know hosting can be tricky, so SiteBay provides live support 24/7. Our team makes sure your site stays online and runs fast on our Kubernetes infrastructure.

If you're dealing with WordPress theme or plugin issues, those usually fall outside our standard support scope. But don't worry—we've got plenty of resources to help you figure things out:

- **Tutorials:** Our [Tutorials](/support/) library is packed with troubleshooting guides.
- **Troubleshooting Guide:** Check out the [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting/troubleshooting/) quick start for common connectivity and management fixes.
- **SiteBay Beginner's Tutorial:** The [SiteBay Beginner's Tutorial](/support/platform/billing-and-support/sitebay-beginners-tutorial/) covers the most common questions we get.
- **Quick Answers:** Hit up [Quick Answers](/support/quick-answers/) for fast fixes.
- **SiteBay MCP Server:** Use our AI integrations to help diagnose your code and config.
- **SiteClaw:** Our built-in security and optimization tool catches a lot of common issues automatically.

## Scope of Support

As outlined in our [Terms of Service](https://www.sitebay.org/tos), our support team doesn't help with installing, configuring, or designing your WordPress site (like tweaking themes, plugins, or custom code). We also don't manage configurations outside of **My SiteBay**. If you're building sites for your own clients, we don't provide direct tech support to them.

What we *do* support is the My SiteBay control panel and making sure the underlying AI-native platform and Kubernetes network are running perfectly.

## Contacting SiteBay Support

Tried the docs and still stuck? Reach out to us. Just remember the [scope of support](#scope-of-support) mentioned above. The best way to get help is to open a ticket in My SiteBay:

1. Log in to [My SiteBay](https://my.sitebay.org).
2. Click **Support** in the sidebar.
3. Hit **New Ticket**.
4. Drop a short **Subject** for the issue.
5. (Optional) Enter the **Domain** you're having trouble with.
6. Give us the details in the **Description**. The more context you provide, the faster we can help.
7. Click **Create Ticket**.

We'll get back to you ASAP.

If My SiteBay is giving you trouble and you can't open a ticket, shoot us an email at <support@sitebay.org>. You can also call us anytime:

- **+1 (855) ...** (North America toll-free)

If you get our voicemail, leave a detailed message and we'll call you back.

## Reporting Abuse

Notice something sketchy? Use our [Abuse Portal](https://www.sitebay.org/legal-abuse/) to report network abuse, unauthorized access, or spam coming from the SiteBay platform. If you're reporting spam, please include the full email headers. For unauthorized access, toss in the relevant logs.
