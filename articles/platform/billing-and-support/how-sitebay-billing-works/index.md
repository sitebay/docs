title: How SiteBay Billing Works
---

We keep billing straightforward at SiteBay. Since you're running on our AI-native WordPress platform powered by Kubernetes, we measure your usage based on visits, storage, and outgoing bandwidth.

You can manage all your payment methods, check past invoices, and monitor your current usage directly in the **Billing** section of [My SiteBay](http://my.sitebay.org). If you have questions about specific charges, you can always ask the SiteBay MCP Server or check out our other billing guides.
